# Open fire and EMS grants, Oct 2026

Funding programs for US fire and EMS departments that were open when checked on October 13, 2026, taken from the issuing agencies' own pages. Dates and rules are the agencies'. Confirm on the linked page before you apply.

## Open when checked

### 1. Illinois Small Equipment Grant, FY2027 (Office of the State Fire Marshal)
- Up to $50,000 per award for small firefighting and ambulance equipment, $6 million in total. Applications due 11:59 pm, November 30, 2026.
- Eligible Illinois government and nonprofit applicants. Check the current notice of funding opportunity for your department's eligibility.
- Applicants need full SAM.gov registration and registration with the Illinois GATA portal. Applicants assigned an FDID must meet applicable NFIRS/NERIS reporting requirements for October 2024 through September 2026. The State Uniform Grant Application and the Small Equipment Grant Application must both be completed electronically.
- Sources: https://sfm.illinois.gov/currentfocus/fy-27-small-equipment-grant-.html and https://sfm.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/sfm/sfmdocuments/documents/FY27-small-equipment-grant-Notice-of-Funding-Opportunity.pdf

### 2. Iowa Volunteer Fire Assistance grant, 2026 (Iowa DNR Forestry)
- 50 percent reimbursement, up to $4,500 per department, for wildland fire needs. Communities of 10,000 or fewer. Applications due November 2, 2026. A valid UEI and an IRS W-9 are required.
- The DNR landing page advertises 2026 applications, but the linked rules and application showed 2025 dates when checked on October 13. Confirm the correct current packet and forms with the DNR before applying.
- Sources: https://www.iowadnr.gov/news-release/2026-10-06/2026-volunteer-fire-department-wildland-fire-assistance-grants-available and https://www.iowadnr.gov/programs-services/forestry-resources/fire-management-safety/fire-prevention

### 3. USDA Community Facilities Direct Loan and Grant Program (rural areas of 20,000 or fewer residents)
- Fire stations, fire trucks and equipment are listed examples of eligible public safety projects. Applications are accepted on an ongoing basis, October 1 to September 30. Contact your USDA Rural Development state office first.
- This is a loan and grant program, not grant-only funding. Eligibility, grant share, terms and available funds vary. Ask the state office about your actual project.
- Source: https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/community-facilities/community-facilities-direct-loan-grant-program
